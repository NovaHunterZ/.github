## 📜 Server Rules

### General Rules

1. **No Cheating** - VAC bans, hacks, or exploits result in permanent ban
2. **No Racism/Discrimination** - Zero tolerance policy
3. **No Mic/Chat Spam** - Keep communication relevant
4. **Respect Admins** - Their decisions are final
5. **No Team Killing** - Intentional TKs result in kicks/bans
6. **English Primary** - English in main chat for moderation

### Ban Appeals

- Check your ban: [SourceBans](http://5.135.143.217/sourcebans)
- Appeal on Discord: [#ban-appeals](https://discord.gg/2DjsQ4xdd5)
- Provide: SteamID, ban date, and reason you believe ban should be lifted
